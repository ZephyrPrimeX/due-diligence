Privacy Policy

Last updated October 30, 2026

DRAFT for review. This is a plain-language starting point, not legal advice. Please review it (ideally with a lawyer) before launching publicly.

Due Diligence is made by Zephyr Labs ("Zephyr Labs", "we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains what the app stores and how it's used.

The short version

  • Due Diligence stores what you type in: your email, an optional first name, and the debt accounts and settings you add. It also keeps simple usage counts (like "an account was added") that never include amounts.
  • Payment reminder emails are on by default (3 days before a due date). You can change or switch them off in Settings or from any reminder email.
  • We don't sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for ads.
  • The app is view only. It never moves money or pays bills. If you choose to link an account, we get read-only data through Plaid and never see your bank login.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time from Settings.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, a securely hashed password (we never see the actual password), and a first name if you give one.
  • Information you enter: account names, lenders, balances, interest rates, minimum payments, due dates, pay-later plans, and app settings such as theme or reminder preferences.
  • Usage counts: when you sign up, open the app (once a day at most), add an account, mark a payment as paid, update a balance, load sample data or send feedback, we record the event name, the time, and which login it came from. These records never contain balances, amounts, account names or lenders. We use them only to count things like sign-ups and weekly active testers so we can improve the app during the beta.
  • Feedback you send: your message, the category you pick, and, if you leave "Include app info" ticked, the app version, your device type, browser and the screen you were on.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting and login provider keeps standard security logs (such as IP address, browser type and sign-in times) to keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not collect bank logins, card numbers, Social Security numbers, or your contacts. Email import and screenshot scanning are marked "Coming soon" and aren't active. Accounts are entered by you, linked by you through Plaid (see below), or clearly labelled sample data.

Linking accounts with Plaid

Account linking is optional and currently in a limited test (it only connects to Plaid's test banks). If you use it, we use Plaid Inc. to connect to your bank, card issuer or loan servicer. You sign in to your institution inside Plaid's own secure window: we never receive or store your login credentials. Plaid's handling of your information is described in the Plaid End User Privacy Policy; by linking an account you agree to it.

For each credit card and loan you link, we receive and store: the account name and the last 4 digits (mask), the institution's name, current and statement balances, credit limit, APRs, minimum or regular payment, next due date, and last payment amount and date. We also receive a count of checking/savings accounts at that institution, but we don't store their balances or any transactions. Plaid gives us an access token for the connection, which we keep encrypted on our servers and never send to your device.

We refresh linked accounts about once a day, when you tap Refresh, or when Plaid tells us new data is available. We use this data only to show your balances, due dates and payoff plans and to send the reminders you turn on. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

To disconnect, open the linked account in the app and tap Disconnect. You can keep the accounts as manual ones or delete them. Either way we ask Plaid to revoke our access and delete our access token. Deleting your Due Diligence account disconnects every link too. You can also manage connections Plaid knows about at my.plaid.com.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to sign you in, save your accounts, show your totals, timeline, payoff plans and insights, send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset, send the payment reminders and summaries you've chosen, and read and reply to feedback you send. We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Where it's stored and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and login provider, on servers in the United States. It is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest. Database rules ensure each signed-in user can only read and change their own rows. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages, and emails (sign-up confirmation, password reset, payment reminders, and feedback you send to our team) go through Resend, which receives your email address and the message itself. Reminder emails include the lender, amount and due date of the payment so you know what's coming.

A copy of your data is also kept on your own device so the app can show it when you're offline. Logging out removes that copy.

We only share information if required by law, or with service providers (like Supabase, Resend and, if you link an account, Plaid) who process it on our behalf to run the app.

Reminder emails

New accounts get reminder emails 3 days before each payment you've entered is due, sent around 9 AM Eastern. In Settings → Reminders you can pick other days (1, 3, 5 or 7 days before), add a daily or weekly summary, choose which account types to include, or switch reminders off. Every reminder email also has an unsubscribe link that turns them off in one step. Sample accounts never trigger reminders.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Delete account in the app. This permanently erases your login and every account, setting, reminder record and feedback message you've saved, right away. Usage counts are kept only as anonymous totals: the link to your login is removed. Linked accounts are disconnected from Plaid at the same time. You can also remove individual accounts at any time, or remove the sample data on its own. Backups held by our provider are cycled out on their normal schedule.

Cookies and local storage

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your theme, and save an offline copy of your data.

Children

Due Diligence isn't intended for anyone under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from children.

Changes and contact

If we make meaningful changes to this policy, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app. Questions or requests? Contact Zephyr Labs at support@zephyrlabs.app.
